# Public Transport

Public transport in and around Belgium, and how to plan a trip. Part of [[Everyday Life]].

**Who runs what**
- **SNCB / NMBS:** national trains (belgiantrain.be and the SNCB app). Live delays and platform changes.
- **STIB / MIVB:** metro, tram and bus in Brussels (stib-mivb.be).
- **De Lijn:** bus and tram in Flanders (delijn.be).
- **TEC:** bus in Wallonia (letec.be).
- **Brussels Airport:** train from Brussels-Airport-Zaventem station; bus lines also run there.

**Planning a trip**
- Google Maps in transit mode combines trains, metro, tram and bus with live delays.
- The SNCB app is the best source for train delays, cancellations and platforms.
- For several operators in one journey, plan it in Google Maps, then check the train part in the SNCB app.
- Allow a margin for changes at Brussels-Midi, Brussels-Central and Brussels-North: platform changes are common.

**Tickets and tips**
- Buy and show tickets in the operator app. Validate on the metro or tram where required.
- A train in Belgium can be late: leave 10 to 15 minutes spare for an appointment or a flight connection.
- Strikes are announced a few days ahead on the operators' sites and on the news; check before an important trip.
- Prices and passes change often, so look them up on the operator site instead of trusting an old note.
