# Google Search

How to search well when Jarvis does not have the answer in the notes. Part of [[Everyday Life]].

**What Jarvis can and cannot do**
- Jarvis answers from [[Brain Graph]] notes plus a few live services ([[Jarvis Services]]: weather, time, prices, news). It does not browse Google by itself.
- When it says "there is no note for it", search yourself, then teach it. On the PC say "remember that ..." and the fact is saved in the notes.

**Search operators that save time**
- `"exact phrase"` finds the words in that order.
- `-word` removes a word. `word1 OR word2` finds either.
- `site:wco.org seizure` searches one website only.
- `filetype:pdf container control` finds PDF reports.
- `intitle:guidelines` needs the word in the page title.
- `after:2026-01-01` limits to newer pages.
- `related:example.com` finds similar sites.

**Good habits**
- Prefer official sources for facts: government and agency sites, UN and WCO pages, company filings.
- Check the date of a page before trusting prices or rules.
- Open Google News for events from the last days, and Google Scholar for studies.
- For law enforcement research keep it to open sources only. See [[LE OSINT Framework]] and [[Seizure Watch]].
