# Chemicals INCB Table I

The 43 substances in Table I of the 1988 UN Convention (INCB Red List, 23rd edition, July 2025): the most closely controlled precursors, including fentanyl precursors and designer pre-precursors. One line per chemical, with CAS, UN and HS numbers, linked drugs, role, legitimate use and what it looks like. Part of [[Precursor Chemicals]].

- **1-Phenyl-2-propanone (P2P / BMK)** - CAS 103-79-7; HS 2914.31; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Amphetamine, Methamphetamine. Role: Direct precursor of amphetamine-type stimulants. Legitimate use: Pharmaceutical (amphetamine, appetite suppressants) and fragrance intermediate. Looks like: Colourless to yellow oily liquid. Also called: Phenylacetone, Benzyl methyl ketone, BMK, P2P, Phenyl-2-propanone.
- **3,4-MDP-2-P methyl glycidate (methyl ester)** - CAS 13605-48-6; HS 2932.99; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: MDMA (ecstasy), MDA. Role: Designer pre-precursor that yields MDP2P (PMK). Legitimate use: No significant legitimate use. Looks like: Off-white / pale solid or oil. Also called: PMK glycidate, PMK methyl glycidate, MMDMG, IPK-1, 3,4-MDP-2-P methyl glycidic acid, methyl ester.
- **3,4-MDP-2-P methyl glycidic acid** - CAS 2167189-50-4; HS 2932.99; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: MDMA (ecstasy), MDA. Role: Designer pre-precursor that yields MDP2P (PMK). Legitimate use: No significant legitimate use. Looks like: White to off-white solid. Note: Salts (e.g. sodium salt) are controlled with different CAS numbers. Also called: PMK glycidic acid, PMK glycidic acid (salts: IPK-2 = sodium salt).

- **3,4-Methylenedioxyphenyl-2-propanone (3,4-MDP-2-P)** - CAS 4676-39-5; HS 2932.92; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: MDMA (ecstasy), MDA, MDEA. Role: Direct precursor of MDMA-type drugs. Legitimate use: Limited fragrance/pharmaceutical intermediate use. Looks like: Pale yellow to amber oily liquid. Also called: MDP2P, PMK, Piperonyl methyl ketone, 1-(1,3-Benzodioxol-5-yl)propan-2-one, 3,4-Methylenedioxyphenylacetone.
- **4-Anilino-N-phenethylpiperidine (ANPP)** - CAS 21409-26-7; HS 2933.36; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Fentanyl and analogues. Role: Late-stage precursor of fentanyl; also a fentanyl impurity/metabolite marker. Legitimate use: Analytical reference standard; very limited legitimate trade. Looks like: White to off-white solid. Also called: 4-ANPP, Despropionyl fentanyl, N-Phenyl-1-(2-phenylethyl)piperidin-4-amine, USP Fentanyl Related Compound E.
- **4-Piperidone** - CAS 41661-47-6 (also 40064-34-4 4-Piperidone monohydrate hydrochloride); HS 2933.39; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Fentanyl and analogues. Role: Early building block for fentanyl precursors (e.g. NPP, 1-boc-4-piperidone). Legitimate use: Pharmaceutical intermediate. Looks like: Usually sold as white crystalline hydrochloride monohydrate. Note: Typically traded as the HCl monohydrate salt (≈65% base) — the free base CAS is rarely seen. Also called: Piperidin-4-one, 4-Oxopiperidine, 4-Piperidone monohydrate hydrochloride (common salt form).
- **Acetic anhydride** - CAS 108-24-7; UN 1715; HS 2915.24; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Heroin, Methaqualone, Amphetamine-type (via P2P). Role: Acetylating agent; also used to convert phenylacetic acid to P2P. Legitimate use: Cellulose acetate, aspirin/paracetamol, dyes, plastics, flavours. Looks like: Colourless, mobile liquid with a sharp vinegar-like smell; fumes in moist air. Note: Most trafficked heroin precursor. Often declared as 'acetic acid anhydride' or mislabelled. Also called: Ethanoic anhydride, Acetyl oxide, Acetic oxide, Acetyl acetate, Anhydride acétique.
- **alpha-Phenylacetoacetamide (APAA)** - CAS 4433-77-6; HS 2924.29; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Amphetamine, Methamphetamine. Role: Designer pre-precursor that yields P2P. Legitimate use: Very limited. Looks like: White to off-white solid. Also called: 2-Phenylacetoacetamide, alpha-Acetyl-alpha-toluamide, BMK intermediate B, Benzeneacetamide, α-acetyl-.
- **alpha-Phenylacetoacetonitrile (APAAN)** - CAS 4468-48-8; HS 2926.40; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Amphetamine, Methamphetamine. Role: Designer pre-precursor that yields P2P. Legitimate use: Very limited. Looks like: White to light-yellow solid. Also called: 2-Phenylacetoacetonitrile, 3-Oxo-2-phenylbutanenitrile, alpha-Acetylphenylacetonitrile.
- **Ephedrine** - CAS 299-42-3 (also 50-98-6 Ephedrine hydrochloride, 134-72-5 Ephedrine sulfate); HS 2939.41; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Methamphetamine, Methcathinone. Role: Direct precursor; salts and pharmaceutical preparations are also controlled. Legitimate use: Decongestant / bronchodilator medicines, hypotension treatment. Looks like: White crystalline powder or colourless crystals (base or salts). Note: Salts have different CAS numbers; a non-listed CAS may still be a controlled salt. Check conversion factors (HCl ≈ 82% base). Also called: l-Ephedrine, (-)-Ephedrine, Efedrina, Ephédrine, Racephedrine (racemic).
- **Ergometrine** - CAS 60-79-7 (also 129-51-1 Ergometrine maleate); HS 2939.61; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: LSD. Role: Ergot alkaloid that can be converted to lysergic acid. Legitimate use: Obstetric medicine (postpartum haemorrhage). Looks like: White to slightly yellow crystalline powder; light sensitive. Also called: Ergonovine, Ergobasine, Ergometrina, Ergométrine.
- **Ergotamine** - CAS 113-15-5 (also 379-79-3 Ergotamine tartrate); HS 2939.62; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: LSD. Role: Ergot alkaloid that can be converted to lysergic acid. Legitimate use: Migraine medicines. Looks like: White to yellowish crystalline powder; light sensitive. Also called: Ergotamina, Ergotamine tartrate (salt).
- **Isosafrole** - CAS 120-58-1; HS 2932.91; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: MDMA (ecstasy), MDA. Role: Precursor converted to MDP2P. Legitimate use: Fragrance and flavour intermediate; insecticide synergists. Looks like: Colourless to pale-yellow oily liquid with anise-like odour. Also called: 5-(1-Propenyl)-1,3-benzodioxole, 1,2-(Methylenedioxy)-4-propenylbenzene, Isosafrol.
- **Lysergic acid** - CAS 82-58-6; HS 2939.63; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: LSD. Role: Direct precursor of LSD. Legitimate use: Essentially none outside research. Looks like: White to off-white crystalline solid. Also called: D-Lysergic acid, 9,10-Didehydro-6-methylergoline-8β-carboxylic acid, Acide lysergique.
- **Methyl alpha-phenylacetoacetate (MAPA)** - CAS 16648-44-5; HS 2918.30; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Amphetamine, Methamphetamine. Role: Designer pre-precursor that yields P2P. Legitimate use: Very limited. Looks like: Colourless to pale-yellow liquid. Also called: Methyl 3-oxo-2-phenylbutanoate, Methyl 2-phenylacetoacetate, alfa-Fenilacetoacetato de metilo.
- **N-Acetylanthranilic acid** - CAS 89-52-1; HS 2924.23; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Methaqualone, Mecloqualone. Role: Direct precursor for quinazolinone sedatives. Legitimate use: Dye and pharmaceutical intermediate (limited). Looks like: White to off-white crystalline powder. Also called: 2-Acetamidobenzoic acid, N-AAA, 2-Acetylaminobenzoic acid, Acide N-acétylanthranilique.
- **N-Phenethyl-4-piperidone (NPP)** - CAS 39742-60-4; HS 2933.37; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Fentanyl and analogues. Role: Key precursor of fentanyl (via ANPP). Legitimate use: Limited pharmaceutical/research use. Looks like: Off-white to yellow solid. Also called: 1-(2-Phenylethyl)piperidin-4-one, 1-Phenethyl-4-piperidone, N-Phénéthyl-4-pipéridone.
- **N-Phenyl-4-piperidinamine (4-AP)** - CAS 23056-29-3; HS 2933.39; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Fentanyl and analogues. Role: Key precursor of fentanyl-type opioids. Legitimate use: Limited pharmaceutical intermediate / reference standard. Looks like: Off-white to tan solid. Also called: 4-Anilinopiperidine, 4-AP, 4-(Phenylamino)piperidine, Despropionyl norfentanyl, USP Fentanyl Related Compound B.
- **Norephedrine (phenylpropanolamine)** - CAS 14838-15-4 (also 154-41-6 Norephedrine / phenylpropanolamine hydrochloride); HS 2939.44; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Amphetamine, Methcathinone-type. Also called: PPA, Phenylpropanolamine, Phenylpropanolaminum, 1-Phenyl-2-amino-1-propanol, Norefedrina.
- **Norfentanyl** - CAS 1609-66-1; HS 2933.39; INCB Table I. Linked drugs: Fentanyl and analogues. Role: Precursor and metabolite/impurity marker of fentanyl. Looks like: Colourless to pale-yellow oily liquid, sassafras (root-beer) smell. Note: Natural oils (sassafras, 'Ocotea' / Cinnamomum camphora 'brown camphor' oils) may contain >80% safrole and should be checked. Also called: 5-(2-Propenyl)-1,3-benzodioxole, 4-Allyl-1,2-methylenedioxybenzene, Shikimol, Sassafras oil (natural source), Safrol.
