# Chemicals INCB Table II

The 8 substances in Table II of the 1988 UN Convention: reagents and solvents with legitimate mass use that are monitored more lightly than Table I. Part of [[Precursor Chemicals]].

- **Acetone** - CAS 67-64-1; UN 1090; HS 2914.11; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: Cocaine, Heroin, LSD, Methamphetamine. Role: Extraction / washing / crystallisation solvent. Legitimate use: Industrial solvent, nail-polish remover, cleaning agent, chemical feedstock. Looks like: Colourless, very volatile, highly flammable liquid with a sweet solvent smell. Also called: 2-Propanone, Dimethyl ketone, Propan-2-one, Acétone, Acetona.
- **Anthranilic acid** - CAS 118-92-3; HS 2922.43; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: Methaqualone, Mecloqualone. Role: Starting material for quinazolinone sedatives. Legitimate use: Dyes, perfumery (methyl anthranilate), UV absorbers, pharmaceuticals. Looks like: White to pale-yellow crystalline powder. Also called: 2-Aminobenzoic acid, o-Aminobenzoic acid, Vitamin L1, Acide anthranilique.
- **Ethyl ether (diethyl ether)** - CAS 60-29-7; UN 1155; HS 2909.11; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: Cocaine, Heroin, LSD. Role: Extraction / purification solvent. Legitimate use: Laboratory and industrial solvent, starting fluid, formerly anaesthetic. Looks like: Colourless, extremely flammable, volatile liquid with characteristic sweet smell; forms explosive peroxides on storage. Also called: Diethyl ether, Ether, Ethoxyethane, Sulfuric ether, 1,1'-Oxybisethane.
- **Hydrochloric acid** - CAS 7647-01-0; UN 1789; HS 2806.10; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: Cocaine, Heroin, Methamphetamine. Role: Acid used to form and crystallise drug hydrochloride salts. Legitimate use: Metal pickling, pH control, food and water treatment, chemical manufacturing. Looks like: Colourless to light-yellow fuming corrosive liquid with pungent odour. Note: Salts of hydrochloric acid are specifically excluded from Table II. Gaseous HCl has the same CAS. Also called: Muriatic acid, Spirit of salt, Hydrogen chloride (aqueous), Chlorhydric acid, Salzsäure.
- **Methyl ethyl ketone (MEK)** - CAS 78-93-3; UN 1193; HS 2914.12; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: Cocaine, Heroin. Role: Extraction / purification solvent. Legitimate use: Paints, coatings, adhesives, cleaning, plastics. Looks like: Colourless, flammable liquid with sweet, acetone-like odour. Also called: 2-Butanone, Butanone, Ethyl methyl ketone, MEK, Méthyléthylcétone.
- **Piperidine** - CAS 110-89-4 (also 6091-44-7 Piperidine hydrochloride); UN 2401; HS 2933.32; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: PCP (phencyclidine). Role: Starting material for PCP. Legitimate use: Pharmaceutical and rubber-chemical intermediate, solvent, base. Looks like: Colourless, flammable, strongly alkaline liquid with ammoniacal / pepper-like odour. Note: Do not confuse with 4-piperidone (Table I) or piperine (pepper alkaloid). Also called: Hexahydropyridine, Azacyclohexane, Pentamethyleneimine, Piperidina.
- **Sulphuric acid** - CAS 7664-93-9; UN 1830/1831/2796; HS 2807.00; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: Cocaine, Heroin, Methamphetamine, MDMA. Role: Strong acid used in extraction, conversion and refining steps. Legitimate use: Fertiliser, batteries, metal processing, chemical manufacturing. Looks like: Colourless, oily, dense, highly corrosive liquid; causes severe burns. Note: UN1830 = acid >51%; UN1831 = fuming; UN2796 = battery fluid (<51%). Salts of sulphuric acid are excluded. Also called: Sulfuric acid, Oil of vitriol, Battery acid, Acide sulfurique, Schwefelsäure.
- **Toluene** - CAS 108-88-3; UN 1294; HS 2902.30; INCB Table II. Linked drugs: Cocaine, Heroin, Methamphetamine, MDMA. Role: Extraction / reaction solvent. Legitimate use: Paints, thinners, adhesives, fuel additive, chemical feedstock. Looks like: Colourless, flammable liquid with sweet, benzene-like odour. Also called: Methylbenzene, Toluol, Phenylmethane, Toluène, Tolueno.
