# Chemicals by Country

Chemicals that a country or region controls nationally in addition to INCB Table I and II, as researched in [[Precursor Chemical ID]] (data as of 2026-10-28). Chemicals a country controls nationally that are NOT in INCB Table I or II (1988 Convention). Built from national legislation and official notices, with a source for every entry. Countries without an entry have no consolidated public list that could be verified. Coverage is "verified" (full national list read), "partial" or "none" (no public list found); missing data is not proof of no controls. Always check current national law. Part of [[Precursor Chemicals]].

The European Union list applies in all 27 member states, including Belgium and the Netherlands (27 EU member entries share it). Country codes are ISO 3-letter codes.
