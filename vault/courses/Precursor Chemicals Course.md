# Precursor Chemicals Course

An Articulate-style interactive e-learning course on precursor chemicals, in English and French, in the PCCP logo and colour style ([[PCCP House Style]]). It lives inside the [[Precursor Chemical ID]] repo (folder docs/course) and is published with the [[GitHub Pages Workflow]].

- **Link:** https://glretief-ux.github.io/Precursor-Chemical-ID/course/ . Add #en or #fr to the end to pick the language.
- **Learners** enter their full name first, can save progress, and get a certificate with the date completed. Nobody needs a Claude account.
- **Videos:** two videos are embedded, English and French (the French version with Madame Sandra).
- **Results:** a trainer who sends the link can collect the results of their own learners through a Google Form linked to a Google Sheet (set in config.js). Without a Form, the learner gets a button to copy or email their result.
- **Content:** the precursor information in [[Precursor Chemicals]].
