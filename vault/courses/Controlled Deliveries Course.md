# Controlled Deliveries Course

**What it is:** a five-day UNODC-WCO Container Control Programme (PCCP) training course with four timed sessions per day.

## Agenda
1. Foundations and legal framework
2. Planning, coordination and cooperation
3. Operational techniques
4. Special considerations and legal process
5. Practical application and evaluation

## How it is built
- Every session is its own comprehensive PowerPoint, sized to fill its time slot, with the full explanation in the speaker notes.
- Built on the house template ([[PCCP House Style]]).

## Decisions
- One standalone deck per session rather than one big deck, so sessions can be reused.
