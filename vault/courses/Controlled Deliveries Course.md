# Controlled Deliveries Course

Five-day UNODC-WCO Container Control Programme training, four timed sessions per day:

1. Foundations and legal framework
2. Planning, coordination and cooperation
3. Operational techniques
4. Special considerations and legal process
5. Practical application and evaluation

Each session is a standalone PowerPoint with speaker notes in the [[PCCP House Style]]. Sister course: [[Cultural Heritage Course]]. Part of [[Training Courses]].
