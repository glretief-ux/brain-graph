# Whisky Lovers Society

**What it is:** a whisky-review YouTube channel, https://youtube.com/@whiskyloverssociety, started in 2021. A "1000 videos" celebration video tasted 10 whiskies as a thank-you (Oct 2026).

## How it works
- Edited in [[DaVinci Resolve]] (paid version, version 21) on an iPad.
- On the Windows PC the plan is Claude Code with a free Resolve MCP bridge so Claude can edit there ([[Claude Code]]).
- Standing look for videos: colour graded, colourful motion graphics with [[Remotion]], something happening on screen at least every 10 seconds, b-roll and photos.
- Every video also gets SEO titles, description and tags.

## Decisions
- Free MCP add-on route chosen over a paid one.
