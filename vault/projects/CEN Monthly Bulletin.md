# CEN Monthly Bulletin

**What it is:** a one-page A4 bulletin of the previous month's seizures.

## How it works
- A scheduled task ("CEN Monthly Seizure Bulletin") produces it on the 1st of every month.
- Output is saved in the bulletins/ folder of the private CEN data repo on [[GitHub]].
- Data comes from [[WCO CEN nCEN Data]] through [[CEN Seizure Analysis]]; branding follows [[PCCP House Style]].
