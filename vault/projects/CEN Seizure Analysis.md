# CEN Seizure Analysis

Excel and Power BI analysis of [[WCO CEN nCEN Data]] (operation CONTAINERcomm 3).

- Workbook with dashboard, free-text search, risk register and anomaly analysis.
- Case-level risk flags rolled into a score and band: [[Risk Scoring]].
- Country studies: Tunisia, six MENA countries, Sri Lanka, each with a PowerPoint brief or dashboard.
- Quarterly updates; [[Power BI]] free tier, so a single Power Query paste keeps it simple.
- Became the live [[CEN Seizure Risk Board]] and the [[CEN Monthly Bulletin]].
- Exports are stored in a private [[GitHub]] repo (CEN-nCEN-seizure-data).
- Also covers the [[UNODC-WCO Open Source Narcotics Data]] dashboard.
