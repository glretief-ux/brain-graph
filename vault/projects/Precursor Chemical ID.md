# Precursor Chemical ID

Public repo and [[GitHub Pages Workflow]] site: a law-enforcement tool covering drug precursors and other chemicals used in illicit manufacture, including all INCB chemicals.

- Identify a chemical by CAS number or UN number and see what it can be used for.
- Search whether a chemical is produced by the company on the sticker.
- Explains how chemicals are controlled from manufacture to importer, which permits are needed and how to check them.
- Professional look: PCCP logo at top, Verdana, white background with blue, laid out like [[Seizure Watch]] ([[PCCP House Style]]).
- Hazardous chemical news category in [[Seizure Watch]].
