# Seizure Watch

Daily open-source news monitor for illegal drug seizures, with risk analysis for law enforcement officers. Live as a [[GitHub Pages Workflow]] dashboard (repo Seizure-watch-).

- Runs daily on [[GitHub]] Actions and publishes a dashboard.
- Multilingual (Arabic, Turkish, Russian, Chinese, Hindi, Vietnamese for ivory) with automatic translation into English.
- Categories include CITES timber, elephant ivory, protected fauna and hazardous chemicals (see [[CITES Timber ID]], [[Precursor Chemical ID]]).
- Risk score uses capped dimensions so seizure weight cannot decide alone: [[Risk Scoring]].
- Views counted with [[GoatCounter]].
- Daily 10am top-10 bulletin and Monday weekly routes report in the [[PCCP House Style]].
- Score was tested against [[WCO CEN nCEN Data]] via [[CEN Seizure Analysis]].

Its look is the model for other dashboards.
