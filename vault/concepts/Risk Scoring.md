# Risk Scoring

Weighted flags rolled into a score and a High, Medium or Low band, with weights as editable numbers.

- [[CEN Seizure Analysis]]: case-level flags such as concealment, multi-country route, controlled delivery and quantity outliers.
- [[Seizure Watch]]: five capped dimensions so seizure weight cannot decide alone, with a "How risk is scored" explanation.
- Also drives the [[CEN Seizure Risk Board]].
