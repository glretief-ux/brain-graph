# Risk Scoring

**What it is:** the common way the projects turn many signals into a High / Medium / Low rating.

## The method
- Several yes/no flags (concealment method, multi-country route, controlled delivery, unusual quantity and similar) each carry a weight.
- Weights add up to a score; thresholds turn the score into a band.
- Weights and thresholds are plain editable numbers, never hidden in formulas.

## Two versions
- [[CEN Seizure Analysis]] scores individual cases and feeds the [[CEN Seizure Risk Board]].
- [[Seizure Watch]] scores news reports on five capped dimensions; seizure weight is limited to 10 points so size alone never decides, and the dashboard explains the scoring in a "How risk is scored" box.

## Principle
Explain every score on screen so an officer can see why something is rated High.
